About

flightlah

flightlah is a free, ad-free Changi and Seletar flight board and live plane tracker. A hobby project built in Singapore.

FAQ

Is this official?
No. Not affiliated with Changi Airport Group or any airline.
Where's the data from?
Public flight info and open ADS-B feeds for live plane positions.
How fresh is it?
The board updates about every 10 min and the map about every 15 sec. You can also refresh manually. Always check with your airline before heading out.
Is it free?
Yes. No ads, no sign-up.
